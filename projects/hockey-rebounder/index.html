A hockey rebounder that passes backA hockey rebounder that passes back

Designed at 14 when lockdown stopped training: a rebounder that returns the ball like a teammate’s pass, now sold in a few German shops.

    01

    From lockdown to shop shelves

    • Problem

      No one to pass to

      Lockdown stopped team training. I could practise alone, but not receiving a real pass.

    • Design

      Tuning the rebound

      I tested foam and rubber combinations until the ball came back like a teammate’s pass.

    • Outcome

      In shops

      I pitched it to a Berlin hockey shop; it now sells in a few shops in Germany. I later founded an online field hockey equipment business.
