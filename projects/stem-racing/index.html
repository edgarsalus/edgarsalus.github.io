Founding Berlin’s first STEM Racing teamFounding Berlin’s first STEM Racing team

I founded and led a six-student team that designed and built a CO₂-powered model F1 car, then made it 19% faster for the German Finals.

Role
Founder, team lead & aerodynamics engineer
Timeline
Nov 2024 – May 2025
Team
Six students from five countries
Tools
Autodesk Fusion, Ansys Fluent, FluidX3D, SLS printing
  • 19%Faster, regional to national car (1.410 s → 1.140 s)
  • −21.5 gSaved by switching to SLS-⁠printed nylon
  • 1stBerlin state champion and Best Newcomer
  • 11thAt the German Finals, 1.155 s race time

    01

    The challenge

    A CO₂ cartridge launches a model F1 car down a 20 m track. Teams are also judged on an engineering portfolio, a business plan and a stand.

    02

    Physics first

    Before redesigning, we modeled the run. The cartridge pushes for about half a second; then the car coasts. Mass mattered more than aerodynamics.

    Aerodynamics

    Three levels of simulation, then checks in a self-built wind tunnel and on track with slow-motion video.

    1. 01

      Concepts

      FluidX3D

      GPU runs to compare shapes fast.

    2. 02

      Quick checks

      CNN surrogate

      Up to 15% faster turnaround.

    3. 03

      Validation

      Ansys Fluent

      Rotating wheels, 3.2 M cells, SST k-ω.

    05

    Wheels and bearings

    The five-cutout wheel had the lowest inertia that still met the stiffness target. Full-⁠ceramic 3×9×3 mm bearings (0 / −0.008 mm) beat glass-bead ones (0 / −0.15 mm) on tolerance.

    06

    Manufacturing

    Switching from SLA resin to SLS-printed PA12 nylon saved 21.5 g, more than the mandatory 7.1 g halo added.

    • SLA

      Setup
      Form 3B, Accura Xtreme resin
      Accuracy
      ±0.10 mm

      Cracked and deformed.

    • FDM

      Setup
      Raise3D Pro2
      Accuracy
      ±0.3–0.5 mm

      Fit tests only.

    • SLS

      Chosen
      Setup
      3D Systems at FastPart Berlin, PA12 nylon
      Accuracy
      ±0.10 mm

      No supports, up to 70% powder reuse.
